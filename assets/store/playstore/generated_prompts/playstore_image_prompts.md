# Google Play Store Image Prompts — Ustyle

This document outlines the purpose, dimensions, design direction, and exact generation prompts for Ustyle's Google Play Store listing assets.

## 1. Phone Screenshots (1080 x 2400 px, Aspect Ratio 9:16)

### Screenshot 1: Value Proposition (Dashboard)
* **Purpose**: Capture user attention instantly by showing the daily outfit recommendations dashboard.
* **Headline**: "Your Closet, Smartly Curated"
* **Subtext**: "Get personalized daily outfit suggestions from your own wardrobe."
* **Image Generation Prompt**:
  > A high-resolution Google Play Store listing screenshot, size 1080x2400. Deep navy-to-slate grey gradient background (`#0F1729` to `#1E293B`). At the top, bold white modern Inter font reads: "Your Closet, Smartly Curated" with a smaller subtitle: "Get personalized daily outfit suggestions from your own wardrobe." In the center, a premium modern Android smartphone frame (Google Pixel) displaying the Ustyle app dashboard: clean white background, a weather card suggesting "Today's Outfit: Smart Casual", a grid of clothing items (beige sweater, black trousers, white sneakers), and a floating dark-navy bottom navigation bar. Creative, modern, professional App Store listing design, 8k, sharp focus.

## 2. Feature Graphic (1024 x 500 px)

### Asset 1: Main Feature Banner
* **Purpose**: Primary branding banner shown on the Google Play Store web and app listing.
* **Image Generation Prompt**:
  > A professional Google Play Store Feature Graphic, size 1024x500. Rich dark blue and slate background (`#0F1729` to `#1E293B`) with abstract soft-glowing lines. On the left side, the Ustyle app logo (a modern, minimalist abstract clothes hanger shape) next to the app name "Ustyle" in a sleek white modern sans-serif font. Below the name, a clear value statement: "Your Personal Digital Closet & Style Assistant". On the right side, two premium modern smartphones angled slightly, showing the clean closet screen and the daily recommendation screen. High-end marketing banner, clean, minimal, professional, Google Play Store branding guidelines.
