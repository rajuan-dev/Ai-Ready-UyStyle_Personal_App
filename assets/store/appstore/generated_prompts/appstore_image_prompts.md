# Apple App Store Image Prompts — Ustyle

This document outlines the purpose, dimensions, design direction, and exact generation prompts for Ustyle's Apple App Store listing assets.

## 1. iPhone 6.5" Screenshots (1284 x 2778 px, Portrait)

### Screenshot 1: Value Proposition (Dashboard)
* **Purpose**: Showcase the core daily dashboard recommendations.
* **Headline**: "Your Closet, Smartly Curated"
* **Subtext**: "Get personalized daily outfit suggestions from your own wardrobe."
* **Image Generation Prompt**:
  > A high-resolution App Store listing screenshot, size 1284x2778. Elegant deep navy-to-slate grey gradient background (`#0F1729` to `#1E293B`). At the top, bold white modern Inter font reads: "Your Closet, Smartly Curated" with a smaller subtitle: "Get personalized daily outfit suggestions from your own wardrobe." In the center, a premium modern iPhone 15 Pro mockup displaying the Ustyle app dashboard: clean white background, a weather card suggesting "Today's Outfit: Smart Casual", a grid of clothing items (beige sweater, black trousers, white sneakers), and a floating dark-navy bottom navigation bar. Creative, modern, professional App Store listing design, 8k, sharp focus.
